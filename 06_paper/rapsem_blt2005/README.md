# RAPSEM–BLT 2005 manuscript

Keep the editable LaTeX source for the BLT 2005–2006, IFLS3/4/5, latent CES-D mediation project here when manuscript files are integrated.

Research questions:
1. What is the total effect (ATT) of BLT on later economic welfare and poverty exit among baseline-poor treated individuals?
2. Under justified C1/C2/S1 assumptions and a validated observational extension, what are the RAPSEM controlled direct and mediator effects?

Do not insert estimated numbers or interpretation until the local variable audit, poverty construction, and identification diagnostics are completed.
